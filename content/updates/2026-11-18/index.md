Well, I thought it was time for another logo refresh. The previous version was intentionally as simple as I could make it, using the fewest possible shapes to represent the letterforms _t_ and _b_.

This version is still based on those same letterforms, but introduces a bit more visual interest while maintaining simplicity. I also wanted the mark to be symmetrical, reflecting the importance I place on order, harmony, and consistency.

I chose this particular design from several alternatives because I like how it also forms a cross, reflecting the fact that my faith in Jesus is the most important aspect of my life. I think it represents me well.
